The Method

Every pass makes the team better

Three passes. Same team, higher standard each time. That's iteration, and it's how every engagement runs.

Pass One

Build

Start with the fundamentals everyone controls. Effort, attitude, showing up for each other. We set the standard together, out loud.

Pass Two

Improve

Push past comfortable. Physical challenges and team problems that show how your people really operate, and what they can handle.

Pass Three

Refine

Debrief it, tie it to the day job, and leave with a standard your team keeps repeating. Then run it again, better.

Relentless improvement. That's the name.
